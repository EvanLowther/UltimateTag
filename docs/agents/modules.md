# Module reference

Every module and its job (moved out of AGENTS.md, which keeps the game flow and the rules). Read the row before changing a module, and keep the row true when you change what it does.


| Module | Side | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `shared/Config.luau` | both | All tunables: tag range, freeze time, speeds, colors, font |
| `shared/BodyRig.luau` | both | Builds the character body: a smooth rounded cartoon person (Crab Game style) from round shapes. Cylinder limbs turned upright through their attachments (joint frames keep standard axes), ball joints, ball head and hands, face (eyes, pupils, mouth), hair, tracksuit stripes, zipper and soles welded on as details. Sizes and colors in `Config.Body`. |
| `shared/Remotes.luau` | both | The RemoteEvents in `ReplicatedStorage.Remotes`: `setup()` on the server, `get(name)` anywhere. `TagSwing` (client to server), `TagSwingPlayed` (server to all clients), `TagKnockback` (server to the player hit: their push), `Notify` (server to one client), `DevCommand` (dev panel, both ways), `PopUp` (client to server: a pop-up's pad threw you), `GameOver` (server to each client when a game ends: the podium, the duration and that player's own rewards; the old `Victory` remote is gone). |
| `shared/Contestant.luau` | both | Players and bots alike: a bot is an ObjectValue in `ReplicatedStorage.Bots` (Name, Value = its character) carrying the same attributes a player has (Role, InGame, Alive, Spectating...) plus `DisplayName` and a negative `UserId`, so code that reads attributes works on either and a bot can go through a remote. `all()` (players then bots), `bots()`, `isBot`, `character`, `displayName`, `userId`, `present`, `byUserId`, `fromCharacter`. Game code that loops over or looks up contestants uses these, never `Players:GetPlayers()` or `.Character` directly. |
| `server/Bots.luau` | server | The bots' lives (`Config.Bots`): `fill(count)` (makes or removes bots from `Names`; bots in a game go last), `add`, `remove`, `removeAll` (also when the last player leaves), `loadCharacter(bot, at)` (a copy of the StarterCharacter, scripts removed, in its `Colors`, a BOT tag and name over its head, owned by the server; respawns after `Game.RespawnTime` when it dies), `knockback(bot, push)` (a hit's push as a fading planar drive, like client/Knockback; `KnockbackStun`), `state(bot)` (its Animate animations, stun), `onCharacterAdded`, `onRemoved` (Roles, Spectators, MapRespawn, Participants and BotBrain listen). |
| `server/BotBrain.luau` | server | What the bots do, on the server. Every frame: steer toward the next path point or straight at a spot it can see (`Humanoid:Move`), sprint at its role's speed, slide (lower hips, the `Sliding` collision group, a `BotMove` attribute the clients pose), play the Animate animations, hold a launch pad's throw until it lands, get unstuck (jump, then a new path or a new spot), and on maps that can kill (`KillHeight`, `FallEliminates`, a train) never step off a drop deeper than `DangerDrop`. Every `Think`: It chases (the nearest Runner, sticking to one for a moment, aiming `Lead` ahead; straight when it can see them on its level, else a PathfindingService path with jumps and ladders, made again every `PathRefresh` (kept while it climbs); a Runner on another level is chased to the floor right under them, so the path goes the long way up the ladder, ramp or stairs, and spots round them are tried if the agent doesn't fit right there; a Runner with no way to them is left be for `NoPathTime` while others are left; a roam or flee spot off its level with no way there is dropped for another, never run at from below; swings via `TagService.swing` a `Reaction` after the Runner is within `SwingReach` of the range, sometimes early, `SwingMiss`; a Runner another It is nearer to is left to them, `ShareDistance`, so two Its don't chase one Runner; real players first: a player counts `PreferPlayers` studs nearer, and a bot Runner within `CloseBot` that this It hasn't tagged yet this round counts the same as a player; just tagged, it can't swing for `TaggedPause` (it still chases)); a Runner roams, flees (`FleeDistance` to `CalmDistance`: the open spot furthest from every It, launch pads when cornered; spots near other Runners score lower, `SpreadDistance`/`SpreadWeight`, so they spread out; no camping: one that has stayed within `CampRadius` of a spot for `CampTime` moves on out of that area, to a spawn point across the map if nothing nearer is out of it; roaming, it pauses under a second where it gets to) and dodges (`DodgeDistance`, `DodgeChance`: a sidestep, jump or slide); lobby and between rounds: a slow wander; intro and win screen: still. Mode changes wait a reaction time. |
| `client/BotPoses.luau` | client | Plays the slide pose (`Config.Slide.Poses`: drop into the held slide) on any bot whose character has `BotMove` = "Slide" (server/BotBrain), on each screen. |
| `shared/GameState.luau` | both | `Phase`, `TimeLeft`, `Round`, `AliveCount`, `Status`, `Paused` (the dev panel paused the timers) as ReplicatedStorage attributes (plus `Winner` during Victory). Server writes, clients read and listen. |
| `server/Roles.luau` | server | `Role` attribute on each Player (`"It"` / `"Runner"` / nil). Applies speed, freeze and the red It highlight (`DepthMode` Occluded: hidden behind walls), again on respawn. |
| `server/TagService.luau` | server | Handles the `TagSwing` remote from anyone: checks they're not frozen and off `Config.Tag.Cooldown`, tells all clients to play the swipe (`TagSwingPlayed`); It's swing during a round tags a Runner: the one the player's screen picked (client/TagSwing) if they're within `AimRange` plus `Tolerance` here too, else the nearest within `PlayerRange` (a bot's: `Config.Tag.Range`), plus a ping allowance in live servers (`LagTime`/`LagMaxExtra`); a player's It swing that hit nobody stays live for `Window` (a later `TagSwing` with `late` from their screen still tags); any other swing shoves the nearest player in reach (`Knockback.Shove`). Tags freeze nobody. Either way the player hit gets a push away from the swinger (`Config.Knockback`: Base plus PerSpeed of the swinger's speed toward them, up to Max) through `TagKnockback`. During a game only players still in it can swing or be hit. With `Config.Game.DebugLog` it logs every tag (distance, speeds) and every It swing that hit nobody (distance to the nearest Runner). |
| `server/Sandbox.luau` | server | Sandbox mode only: players join as Runners, keeps one player as It |
| `server/Avatar.luau` | server | Makes the `StarterCharacter` (currently the classic 2012 block R15 rig, `Config.Body.Enabled = false`; with `Config.Avatar.UseOwnColors` each player's block body takes their own Roblox body colors; with `UseOwnLook` it also wears their own clothes, accessories and face, applied from their HumanoidDescription with the body parts, scales and animation pack taken out, so everyone keeps the same block body and animations): the BodyRig (with Roblox's standard Animate script borrowed from a classic rig), or the classic 2012 block rig if `Config.Body.Enabled` is off. Respawns any non-R15 character. |
| `server/CollisionGroups.luau` | server | Registers the `SlideBlocker` / `Sliding` collision groups and puts `SlideBlocker` parts in their group |
| `server/GameLoop.luau` | server | The game state machine: Lobby, Countdown, rounds (Intro, Playing, Results), Victory. Or the Sandbox in Sandbox mode. Rounds: placement on a map loaded during the previous Results, Its by `RoundPlan.eliminateTarget`, early end when the target is out, a new random It if none is left, the `RoundResults` card (the next map loads behind it), the AFK check. At the game's end it computes the podium and each player's rewards, sets Victory, respawns everyone in the lobby, unloads the map and fires `GameOver` (the win screen). |
| `server/Spectators.luau` | server | Out of the game = camera only: `eliminated(player)` (the ELIMINATED moment: frozen, red flash for `Config.Game.EliminatedTime`, then parked), `fellOut`, `park` (anchored, invisible, no collision, `Parked` attribute on the character, moved to `Lobby.Position + ParkOffset`; sets the player's `Spectating` attribute), and every body that spawns for a player who is out of a running game (late joiners, the respawn after falling) is parked too. `releaseAll` clears `Spectating` at the game's end. `leaveEarly` (the `LeaveGame` remote): an out player goes back to the lobby now (`LeftEarly` attribute, not parked again). |
| `server/GameLog.luau` | server | `Config.Game.DebugLog` (Studio by default): `say(text)` prints a line tagged with round, phase and time left; `describe`/`everyone` list players with roles and game state. Used by GameLoop, Participants, TagService. |
| `server/GameRewards.luau` | server | What each player earned during one game (coins and crates added up from profile changes, XP as the difference in total XP, new items), for the win screen. Watches `PlayerData.onChanged`; not exact if the player claims something else from the menu during the game. |
| `server/Participants.luau` | server | Who is in the game: `InGame` / `Alive` player attributes, keeps `AliveCount` updated. Records the order players went out in: `podium(limit)` (the winner, then the last out; going out later ranks higher, also within a round; only players still in the server), `placeOf(player)`, `gameId()`. |
| `server/MapService.luau` | server | Map catalog from `ServerStorage.Maps`: `choose(playerCount, lastMap)` by size tier, `load`/`unload` as `Workspace.ActiveMap`, `spawnPoints`. |
| `server/Modes/HotPotato.luau` | server | The Hot Potato game mode (see Game flow). |
| `shared/RoundPlan.luau` | both | Pure pacing functions (no Roblox services): `eliminateTarget(players, round, maxRounds)` (the schedule so a game takes at most 5 rounds; table in its comment), `schedule`, `roundTime(players, bands)`, `initialCountdown` and `countdownAfter` (lobby countdown rules). Checked by `lune run tools/roundplan-test.luau`. |
| `server/RoundStats.luau` | server | Per-round numbers for the results card: tags, times tagged, seconds as It (`reset`, `begin`, `finish`, `tagged`, `get`). |
| `server/Afk.luau` | server | 30 s (`Config.Afk`) without a client `Activity` ping or body movement during the Playing phase puts a player out (GameLoop listens with `onAfk`). Never in the intro, never for players already out. |
| `client/Activity.luau` | client | Sends the throttled `Activity` ping when you press or move something. |
| `client/RoundResults.luau` | client | The results card (`RoundResults` remote): who went out and how, players left, your tags and time as It (only with `Config.Game.ResultsCard`, off since 0.60.0); also the loading cover (a full-screen dark backdrop fades in after `CoverAt` while the next map loads, gone when the intro starts). |
| `client/Spectate.luau` | client | Only in Intro, Playing and Results and only with the `Spectating` attribute (set when the server parks your body): third person (`ViewMode.suspend`), controls off (`ControlLock`), the camera follows a living player; Q/E, arrows or the on-screen prev/next buttons switch. A Leave button (eliminated players only) sends them to the lobby early. Stops the moment the phase leaves those (the game ended) and gives the camera back once the new lobby character is here. Steps aside while the Locker preview has the camera. |
| `client/ControlLock.luau` | client | Turns Roblox's player controls off while any owner (`Spectate`, `WinScreen`) holds a lock; `set(owner, locked)`, `apply()`. |
| `client/WinScreen.luau` | client | The win screen, from the `GameOver` remote (podium, your rewards; its length fits the winner's victory, `Catalog.winScreenTime`): a stage built on your screen only at `Config.WinScreen.Position` with copies of the podium players on 1st/2nd/3rd blocks, the winner's equipped Victory (`client/Victories`, which may move the camera, the blocks and the other copies), a scripted camera, name plates, a headline and your own place, coins, XP, crates and items card. HUD, menu, controls and first person are off while it plays; it ends after `Duration` or when the phase leaves Victory. |
| `client/Sounds.luau` | client | Every sound (Config.Sounds): `play` (2D), `at` (3D at a position, part or attachment), `make` (a Sound for a module to drive), `ui` (a menu sound that replaces that press's click), `victory`/`stopVictory` (a victory's track), `settings`/`set`/`onSettings` (the sliders and mute: two SoundGroups, Music and Sfx, saved through `SaveSettings`). Wires hover and click on every button in the player's ScreenGuis (not the dev panel, not ones with a `Silent` attribute), plays the round's countdowns, whistle and buzzer from GameState and the player's attributes, the tag sounds from `TagHit`, and the background: the lobby's rain and music near the lobby, the map's ambience (`MapAmbience`, by MapName) or the round music near the map, silence on the win screen, unchanged elsewhere (the Locker). Hooks: TagSwing (swings), Victories, Trains, MapGadgets (launches), PopUps, MapAmbience (the spinner), Menu (equip, claims; ShopTab buy and not enough coins; Popups level up and the crate reel). A map's own music plays with its ambience (`MapMusic`); parts with `SoundLoop` play their loop in 3D. |
| `client/Menu/SettingsTab.luau` | client | The menu's Settings page (a speaker icon): Music, Sound effects and UI sliders (click or drag) and Mute all sound. |
| `server/Settings.luau` | server | The `SaveSettings` remote: checks the sound settings and keeps them in the profile's `Settings`. |
| `server/WinsStore.luau` | server | Lifetime wins: kept in the profile (`Stats.Wins`), shown in the Wins column, copied to the all-time top list (OrderedDataStore `TotalWins_v1`); `addWin`, `top(n)` for the leaderboard. |
| `server/LobbyBoards.luau` | server | Fills the lobby's three boards (text parts with `Board`/`Row`/`Field` attributes): GLOBAL LEADERS from `WinsStore.top` and RICHEST PLAYERS from `Leaderboards.top("Coins")` every `Config.Lobby.BoardRefresh` seconds, THIS SESSION (wins and tags since joining, from Progression events, this server's players) every `SessionRefresh`; footers say when saving is off. |
| `server/RewardWheel.luau` | server | The `RewardWheelPrompt` spins the `RewardWheel` part for everyone and pays a weighted prize (`Config.Lobby.WheelPrizes`: coins, XP or a crate, via Progression.grant); one spin per `WheelCooldownHours`, saved in the profile. The Night Market has no wheel, so it waits for one. |
| `client/Notify.luau` | client | Short on-screen messages from the `Notify` remote, and from ProximityPrompts with a `Message` attribute. |
| `server/Leaderstats.luau` | server | `Level` (from the profile), `Wins` (lifetime, from WinsStore) and `Tags` (session) columns in the player list. `add` and `set`. |
| `client/Sprint.luau` | client | Hold `Config.Sprint.Key` (Left Shift) to run `Config.Sprint.Multiplier` times faster. Client-side for instant feel. Works even when Roblox has claimed Shift (a player's Shift Lock setting; the project also turns `EnableMouseLockOption` off). Also the one owner of the camera's field of view, eased every frame: `Config.Camera.FieldOfView` (70 top to bottom) in third person, `Config.FirstPerson.FieldOfViewAcross` (120 left to right, converted for the screen's shape) in first person, plus `FovBoost` while sprinting; hands off while the Locker preview has the camera. |
| `client/Slide.luau` | client | The C key (`Slide.cancel()` ends a running slide or crouch from outside). Sprinting on the ground: slide (boost unless within `DeadSlideTime` = dead slide, and never past sprint speed x `StartMultiplier`, so chained slides can't build speed; uses your landing speed within `LandingGrace`). Moving fast in the air: air slide (no boost, slow-down starts on landing). Otherwise: crouch at `CrouchSpeed` (idle pose, crouch-walk cycle while moving; height worked out from leg lengths so feet stay on the floor), low enough for slide bars, no jumping under a ceiling. A slide that runs out with C held becomes a crouch. Can't stand up under a ceiling; jumping out of a slide keeps its speed. Slopes: downhill speeds a slide up (`SlopeAccel`) and holds off its fade, uphill slows it, and what a slope gave fades on the flat (`SlopeFade`); `MaxSpeed` 90. Jumping out of a slide keeps its speed and goes higher the faster it was (`JumpLift`, up to `JumpLiftMax`); `Slide.jump()` does that from outside (CoyoteJump). Holding C as you land adds `Momentum.LandSlideCarry` of the drop. Moving faster than walk speed on the ground counts as fast (slides out of carried momentum). A slide ending on the ground faster than you run hands its speed to GroundMomentum. |
| `client/CoyoteJump.luau` | client | Coyote time: a jump still works for `Config.Jump.CoyoteTime` (0.15 s) after you run, walk or slide off an edge (off the edge mid-slide it's a slide jump, `Slide.jump()`). Once per time in the air; not after a jump or after a move took over mid-air (wall run, launch, ladder); not while frozen. |
| `client/SlidePose.luau` | client | The slide and crouch animations: builds keyframed animations from `Config.Slide.Poses` (Drop, Slide, Lunge, Crouch from the reference frames, plus CrouchWalk) and plays them through the Animator. Also the hip sink and camera dip. Registered on the client only, so other players don't see it yet; replace with an uploaded Animation before multiplayer. |
| `client/AirMomentum.luau` | client | In the air with no move running, owns sideways velocity while you hold a movement key: keeps your speed (wall runs and slides carry through) and turns it fast toward where you steer relative to your view (`TurnRate` 1080: W your view, A/D to the side), steering back brakes; let go of the keys and the extra speed bleeds off to walk speed (`Momentum.ReleaseDecay`; launch pad flights keep theirs). Fall gain and the landing boost need a key held too. Records the speed you land with for slides. Falling builds speed (`Config.Momentum`): coming down below where you left the ground while moving, `FallGain` of gravity's pull goes into your speed across (up to `MaxAirSpeed`), and landing lower than you took off adds `LandCarry` of the speed that drop is worth (`MoveState.landImpact`); a jump that lands where it started gains nothing (no bunny-hop speed). Every landing keeps only `LandKeep` of the speed carried above your run speed (also when you bounce straight back up with jump held, `LandRebound`), so chained hops and slide-jumps wind down. Launch pad flights gain nothing. |
| `client/GroundMomentum.luau` | client | Carry and fade: ending up on the ground faster than you can run (a landing, a slide ending fast, a knockback) keeps that speed along the ground with a flat drive, fading back to your walk speed at `Momentum.GroundDecay` (frozen players skid to a stop); only while you hold a movement key: it follows where you steer relative to your view (`GroundTurnRate` 1080), steering back brakes, and letting go drops it at once (a knockback's skid, `MoveState.groundSkid`, ignores keys and keeps its direction). Starts only from hand-offs (`landVelocity`, `groundVelocity`), never from ground speed itself (trains can't set it off). Lets go for any move, a jump (hands to AirMomentum), a wall, or at walk speed. |
| `client/Knockback.luau` | client | Applies a hit's push (`TagKnockback`) on the player hit: `AirMultiplier` stronger and a bigger pop if they're in the air, ends a slide or crouch (not ladders, wall runs, launches, train pushes), holds `MoveState.busy = "Knockback"` with a flat drive until they land (a wall stops it), then hands the push to GroundMomentum to skid on and fade. `Knockback.apply(push, pop, airMultiplier)` is the same flight for map hazards (client/MapAmbience). |
| `client/WallRun.luau` | client | Automatic when airborne and fast (sprinting or `MinEntrySpeed`) with a wall within `Reach` beside or diagonally (nearest wins), not moving away from it and not heading within `HeadOnAngle` of straight into it: run along it (following the wall as it curves, rise then sink, camera leans away). Jump during a wall run to wall-jump: up (`JumpUp`) and the way you're looking at the run's speed, never back into the wall (at least `JumpAwayAngle` off it); land on another wall to chain runs across a gap. A chain can't climb more than `ChainMaxRise` above where it started (touching the ground starts a new one), and the next run's lift stops there too. Wall-run lift stops at ceilings (`CeilingStop`). The only wall move: the wall climb, cling and ledge hang were removed in 0.41.0 (and the wall kick-off in 0.33.0). |
| `client/SpeedTrails.luau` | client | Trails on hands and feet for every player (fast, or for you during a move: `Config.SpeedTrails`), each in their equipped trail (`EquippedTrail` attribute) by its look: solid, stripes, camo, two lanes (split, checker), gradient, glow, Legendary particles (embers, stars, sparkles, sparks, snow), Iridescent flowing holo colors plus afterimages. Your hands don't trail in first person. `preview(model)` puts always-on trails on the Locker's copy. |
| `client/MoveState.luau` | client | The one source of movement state: `busy` (one move at a time), `airVelocity` (speed carried through the air), `landVelocity`/`landTime`/`landImpact` (speed you last landed with, and the drop's speed), `groundVelocity` (a hand-off to GroundMomentum), `turnToward()`, `parts()`, `isGrounded()`, `flat()`, `drive()`, `launchTime` (set by launch pads), `cameraYaw()`/`yawChange()`/`turnFlat()` (look coupling), `headroom()` (free space above your head, so upward drives stop at ceilings). New moves read these instead of re-checking. |
| `client/Crouch.luau` | client | The only code that changes HipHeight (and CameraOffset in third person; FirstPersonBody owns it in first person). Moves call `Crouch.set(humanoid, factor, seconds, cameraDip)`. |
| `client/AnimationBuilder.luau` | client | Builds keyframed animations from pose tables (degrees per R15 body part) and plays them. `make(keys, partial, looped)`: each key can set `ease` ("In", "Out", "InOut", "Linear"; it shapes the move to the next key); with `partial`, unlisted parts are left to the animation underneath. `mirror()` flips a pose left/right. Client-only. Roblox can only play code-built animations through the Animator in Studio (`RegisterAnimationClip` throws in a published game), so they always run on a software animator, in Studio too, so Studio looks exactly like a live server: it sets the body's joint transforms every frame (Motor6Ds or AnimationConstraints, through `client/Joints`) (put back in PreAnimation, set in PreSimulation, fading in and out; tracks on one joint blend by weight, laid over the Animator's, like Roblox blends one priority) behind the same track calls (`Play(fade, weight, speed)`, `Stop`, `AdjustSpeed`, `AdjustWeight`, `IsPlaying`, `Looped`, `KeyframeReached`, `Destroy`). `Config.Animation.NativeInStudio` plays them through the Animator instead (Studio only, to compare). `afterAnimation(step, undo)`: code that changes joints after the animations (FirstPersonArms, the victories' `Kit.every`) runs through it, always after the software tracks and undone before them, so the order never changes. Real Roblox animations (the Animate script's idle and run) still go through the Animator. |
| `client/Joints.luau` | client | A character's joints of either kind: Motor6Ds, or the AnimationConstraints Roblox's Avatar Joint Upgrade puts in their place (characters in every game get them now). `of(model)` (cached, found again when joints are swapped) and `find` map each body part's name to the joint that moves it; `part0`, `part1`, `c0`, `c1` (an AnimationConstraint's attachments) and `offset`. Any code that reads or turns joints goes through it, never `IsA("Motor6D")`. |
| `client/RunPace.luau` | client | Every frame sets Roblox's WalkAnim/RunAnim pace from your ground speed, clamped (`Config.RunPace`), so launches, boost landings or crouches can't leave the run animation stuck too fast. |
| `client/Upright.luau` | client | Turns off the FallingDown and Ragdoll humanoid states (no toppling) and `AutomaticScalingEnabled` (otherwise Animate speeds up the run animation whenever a move lowers the hips). The server also sets it on the StarterCharacter. |
| `client/FollowCam.luau` | client | The third-person camera: Roblox's camera in Follow mode (`Config.Camera.Follow`, also set on StarterPlayer) handles input; every frame after it, a sphere sweep (`CollisionRadius`) from the focus out to the camera pulls the camera in front of anything solid at once and eases it back out (`ZoomOutRate`), keeps the focus below ceilings, and fades your character when the camera is pushed close (`FadeDistance`). Never through walls. First person untouched. |
| `client/FaceCamera.luau` | client | Facing. Third person: Roblox turns you toward your running (`AutoRotate`). First person: face the camera. A move can set `MoveState.faceDirection` and the body turns smoothly to it (`Config.Camera.TurnRate`). |
| `client/CameraKick.luau` | client | Camera feel layered on the normal camera each frame: `kick(roll, pitch, duration)` short jolts, `lean(roll)` a held roll (wall running). |
| `client/ViewMode.luau` | client | First or third person. Live servers are first person only; in Studio it starts as `Config.Camera.StartFirstPerson` says and V toggles (`AllowToggle`, Studio only). |
| `client/HeadTags.luau` | client | The tags over heads (titles, the bots' BOT tags: their BillboardGuis' `Content` frame) shrink with distance from the camera like the character does: full size within `Config.HeadTags.FullSizeWithin` (never bigger than designed), down to `Smallest`, so far-off players' tags don't fill the screen. |
| `client/Cursor.luau` | client | The mouse in first person: locked to the screen centre, freed (a Modal button) only while Alt is held or a window holds it (`Cursor.hold`: the menu, the dev panel; in the lobby too if `FreeInLobby`). While it's locked the arrow is hidden (`MouseIconEnabled`) and a hollow aim ring shows at the centre (`Crosshair`; `setHot` turns it red while you're It with a Runner in reach), with a HOLD ALT FOR MOUSE reminder. `Config.Cursor`. |
| `client/FirstPersonBody.luau` | client | First person shows your real body: your arms and legs are drawn as local copies (slimmer: `Config.FirstPerson.ArmThickness`/`LegThickness`) that follow the real limbs every frame, so every third-person animation shows; head, torso and accessories stay hidden; your own highlight is hidden on your screen. Moves the camera with your head via `CameraOffset`, `BodyForward` studs ahead. Designed on the "First Person Mockup" canvas. |
| `client/FirstPersonArms.luau` | client | First person only, after the animations each frame (`AnimationBuilder.afterAnimation`: after the code-built animations, put back before they run): lifts the upper arms (`ArmLift`, measured against the torso's lean, capped at `MaxArmAngle`) and tucks them in (`ArmTuck`), shoulders in (`ShoulderWidth`), so the hands stay in view; during a wall run the wall-side hand drags on the wall (`WallHand`). Client-only joint changes, so others see normal animations. |
| `client/ItTint.luau` | client | First person while you're It: a faint red wash plus four edge gradients (darkest in the corners), fading in and out (`Config.FirstPerson.ItTint`); stands in for your red highlight, which is hidden on your own screen. |
| `client/TagSwing.luau` | client | Left click (or R2), anyone not frozen: plays the swipe at once (seen in first person too), fires `TagSwing` with who your screen says you hit (the player nearest the aim ring within `Config.Tag.AimRange` and `AimAngle`, else the nearest within `PlayerRange`; Runners only while you're It in a round); a swing that found nobody keeps looking for `Window` and sends a late hit. While you're It the aim ring turns red (`AimHotColor`, `Cursor.setHot`) whenever a Runner is in reach. Plays other players' swipes when `TagSwingPlayed` arrives, so everyone sees them. |
| `client/Hud.luau` | client | The top bar (phase tag, timer, players left) and status line (bigger in the lobby and countdown), and banners (YOU'RE IT!, RUN!, ELIMINATED, YOU WIN!, "<name> wins!") that sweep across as a tilted stripe and go (no small tag). The role banner shows only in the first `RoleBannerTime` 3 s of a round's intro, once. |
| `server/MapRespawn.luau` | server | Falling off: kills anyone below the loaded map's `KillHeight`, and players who die on a map during a round (or in Sandbox) respawn on its spawn points instead of the lobby, keeping their role.
