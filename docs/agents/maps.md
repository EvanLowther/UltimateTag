# Maps, lobby, lighting and art

Moved out of AGENTS.md. Read it before working on any file listed in `paths` above (agents that support path-scoped rules load it by themselves).

## Map and world look
- **Studded, simple, classic** for the test map: Plastic material, bright solid colors, `TopSurface = Studs` / `BottomSurface = Inlet`.
- **Real maps** (the School) use Roblox's real materials (CeramicTiles, Plaster, WoodPlanks, Metal, Brick...) and lots of non-colliding detail (posters, signs, pipes) to feel lived in. Detail never collides or blocks raycasts (`CanCollide`/`CanQuery`/`CanTouch` off), so wall runs and the camera ignore it; only deliberate props are solid.
- **The lobby** (the Night Market) is a rainy, neon-lit night market at night: real materials (asphalt, brick, wood, metal), neon signs, lanterns and string lights, and lots of non-colliding detail, built from parts like the maps (`tools/lobby_layout.py`) and designed for first person at eye height (about 5).
- **Lighting** (in `default.project.json`, Rojo-managed) is bright and saturated: ShadowMap, a ColorCorrection boost, soft Bloom, light Atmosphere. Simple white speed trails are the only other effect.

## Maps
Each map is a folder in `src/maps/<MapName>/`:
- `init.meta.json` makes it a `Model` with attributes `DisplayName`, `MinPlayers`, `MaxPlayers` (the player counts it suits: its size tier) and `InRotation` (false keeps it out of games).
- Optional map attributes: `OutOfBounds` and `ReachRoutes` (for `tools/mapcheck.py`, see the geometry rules below), `KillHeight` (world Y: falling below it kills you, and you respawn on the map's spawns; server/MapRespawn), `FallEliminates` (true: dying on the map while a round is being played puts you out of the game instead: you come back in the lobby and spectate; the Rooftop District) and `Lighting` (a preset in `Config.MapLighting.Presets`, like `Night`, shown while your camera is near the map; client/MapLighting). Gadgets are attributes on parts: `LaunchVelocity` (Vector3, a launch pad; sliding or crouching onto it launches you too; a pop-up's pad throws nobody while its toy is out), `LaunchAnyDirection` (true: the pad throws you the way you're moving, with `LaunchVelocity`'s up speed and speed across; the Rooftop District's vents), `PopUp` (a pop-up's name: its pad, the part with `LaunchVelocity`, springs the toy open for everyone when it throws someone, client/PopUps and server/PopUps; the toy's parts share the name and have a `PopRole`, `Lid` (turns `PopAngle` round its hinge line, `PopHinge` and `PopHingeAxis`, an edge it shares with its box), `Head` (rises `PopRise`) or `Spring` (stretches under the head); the pad has the timing, `PopTime`, `PopCooldown` (it throws nobody while the toy is out), `PopBack`, and `PopAt`, set by the server; the Playroom's jack-in-the-box), `Door` (a sliding door's name: its parts carry `DoorSlide`, a Vector3 each slides by when open, and `DoorCenter`, the doorway's middle; it opens while anyone is within `Config.Doors.Range`, server/Doors and client/Doors; mapcheck lets you through `DoorSlide` parts; the Space Station), `SoundLoop` (a sound's name: the part plays it on a loop in 3D, heard from `SoundNear` out to `SoundRange`; client/Sounds), `SpeedBoost` (number, studs/s when you jump or run off it) and `Slip` (number, seconds you're down after slipping on it: you fall back and skid on with your momentum; no map uses it now); client/MapGadgets.
- Pieces are `*.model.json` files, one per part or model, in the studded plastic style. Don't put a `name` field in a top-level model file (the file name is the name).
- A `Spawns/` folder of invisible, non-colliding, anchored parts: where players are placed at the start of a round. Have at least as many as the map's `MaxPlayers` if you can (they're reused if not).
- Maps are built around the origin (the lobby is far away at X=2000).
- Place parts with `CFrame`, never `Position`: `"CFrame": { "CFrame": { "position": [x, y, z], "orientation": [[1,0,0],[0,1,0],[0,0,1]] } }`. `Position` only works during live sync and is lost in the built `.rbxlx`, so everything loads at the origin.
- To add a map, write a generator for it (`tools/*_layout.py` on `tools/maplib.py`) that writes its folder. MapService picks it up automatically. With more players than any map's `MaxPlayers` (over 12 now), MapService picks the closest size tier (the Rooftop District).

- Any part with the `SlideBlocker` attribute blocks standing players but not sliding or crouching ones (CollisionGroups also handles maps loaded later).
- **Ladders** are how players get up anything taller than a jump (about 5.5; there's no wall climb or mantle), used sparingly (the School has one): Roblox characters climb TrussParts by themselves, so `maplib.ladder(...)` stands rails and rungs flat on a face (`gap` 0.07 off it; more on a curved wall, whose flat pieces bow in) with invisible trusses (2 x 2, tops level with the ledge) whose fronts are level with the rails and the rest sunk into the wall, and `client/Ladders` holds `MoveState.busy = "Climb"` while you're on one so no other move fires mid-climb. A ladder needs a solid backing at least 2 deep for its whole height (a wall, or a column under a floor's edge: never standing free in a room, which floats), nothing solid in front of it, and an opening at the top (split any railing).
- **The lobby's footprint is off limits to every map** (`lobby` in `tools/mapcheck.py`: no part of any map, solid or not, may reach into it).
- **Geometry rules (no flicker, no clipping)**, checked by `tools/mapcheck.py` for every map in rotation and the lobby, and by each generator before it writes: no part thinner than 0.001 or with a negative size; no two visible faces in the same plane facing the same way where they overlap (z-fighting: they flicker as the camera moves), and nothing laid less than 0.04 over a surface. So: build stacked steps (bleachers, tiers) as side-by-side blocks, not overlapping ones; walls end at the face of the wall they meet (not its center line); floor finishes and overlays stop at wall faces; decoration sits at least 0.05 off its surface (more off curved walls, whose flat pieces bow in); a part never tops out exactly at a floor's surface unless the floor sits on it; round floors and walls come from `maplib`'s exact pieces (`ring_segment`, `ring_wall`, `ring_floor`), never overlapping boxes; window sill strips sit on the wall's faces; nothing (decoration included) cuts into or sits right in front of window glass; a ladder's invisible truss is sunk into something solid (a floating ladder is flagged). It also checks reach: every walkable area can be reached from the spawns and you can always get back (walking, jumps, drops, crouching under, ladders, launch pads, level swinging platforms; wall runs passed in as routes). Tops that aren't play area (roofs, ceilings, tall furniture) go in the map's `OutOfBounds` attribute (comma-separated part names). **Only the Rooftop District (falling off), the Metro (the train), Canyon Express and the Arctic Icebreaker (falling off) may kill anyone**, so the check fails any other map with a `KillHeight` or `FallEliminates` attribute (`deaths`), and on every map without a `KillHeight` it fails if anywhere reachable (the same walking, jumping, dropping, ladder and launch pad moves as the reach check) has open void beside it to walk off into, or level out-of-bounds ground to step onto (a gap in a wall, a wall top, a jump onto an outside ledge), or a launch pad throws you out of the map (`escapes`); wall runs and wall jumps are not modelled, so a wall climbed that way isn't caught. Moves the check can't work out (a wall run, a slide jump) go in its `ReachRoutes` attribute (`x,y,z>x,y,z` one way, `<>` both ways, `;` between; `mapcheck.routes_attribute` writes it), so the check finds them on the map's folder too.

## Lobby
The lobby is the **Night Market** (since 0.52.0; designed on the "Night Market Lobby" design canvas, then ported): a rainy, neon-lit night market where up to 30 players wait between games, at `Config.Lobby.Position` (2000, 0, 0), far from the maps. **Generated**: change `tools/lobby_layout.py` and run `python3 tools/lobby_layout.py` (rewrites `src/lobby/`, never edit those by hand); Rojo puts it in the game as `Workspace.Lobby` and owns all of it (`$ignoreUnknownInstances` false): connecting Rojo removes anything else in it. A connection that was already open when the project gained the lobby only added it, which left the old island beside it (0.52.1); deleting the old pieces once in Studio fixed that. The generator builds the design round the origin, runs its checks (part budget 5000, nothing hanging under 9 over a walkway, every way through at least 12 wide, board and sign text sizes, the training stations to size, the launch pad landing on its platform, the floodlights aimed, then mapcheck), then wires up the game's copy and moves it to `ORIGIN` (keep it equal to `Config.Lobby.Position`). Tall dark buildings (40-90) close it in all round; only the ground, buildings, stall counters, fences, walls, the arch's pillars, the lantern poles and the training course are solid. `OutOfBounds`: buildings, towers, roof boxes, the outer ground; `Lighting` `NightMarket` (client/MapLighting). How strong the neon looks: `NEON_STRENGTH` in the generator (Neon parts' colours and glowing sign text, 0.56) and the `NightMarket` preset's Bloom in Config.
- **Central plaza** (radius 40): the lantern pole (44 high, paper lanterns, string lights out to 16 lamp posts), a ring of benches, and the **spawn ring**: 16 `SpawnLocation`s (`LobbySpawn`...; invisible, flush with the plaza) on a ring of radius 30, facing north to the boards. GameLoop turns them all off in Sandbox.
- **Leaderboard wall** (a facade at z -70 over a promenade): three boards, **GLOBAL LEADERS** (all-time wins, WinsStore), **THIS SESSION** (this server's players by wins and tags since they joined, kept in memory) and **RICHEST PLAYERS** (most coins: server/Leaderboards' `Coins` board). Their row and footer text parts carry `Board`, `Row` (0 = the footer) and `Field` (`Name`, `Value`, `Wins`, `Tags`, `Footer`) attributes; server/LobbyBoards fills them.
- **Market Street** (east, through the NIGHT MARKET arch): the shop stalls TODAY'S DEAL, TRAILS, TAG EFFECTS, TITLES, VICTORIES and MYSTERY CRATE, each with a ProximityPrompt (E, tagged `AvatarShopPrompt`) that opens the Shop, plus two food stalls; the **food court** at its end; the **noodle bar and lounge** in the west.
- **Training Alley** (south, through the gate): a fenced one-way lap of six signed stations: slide (a 40 sprint lane, a 4.2 slide bar, a crouch tunnel), gap jumps (6, then 11), a wall run (48 x 16), a launch pad (`LaunchVelocity`) onto a platform 16 up, a ladder to a ledge 14 up (the only way on), and three tag dummies (props for now: swings don't do anything to them). Guide fences (6, too high to jump) keep it one way.
- Moving bits: neon flicker (`Flicker`) and fans (`Spin` round a `SpinAxis`), animated by client/MapAmbience, which watches the lobby as well as the loaded map (and parts that stream in later); rain streaks, steam and mist are particles.
- No reward wheel, winners' stage or spectator area (the victory plays on everyone's screen; players spectate on their own screen). The old tropical island's Blender source (`tools/blender/make_lobby.py`, `assets_src/lobby/`) is still in the repo but unused; its Studio setup tool is gone.

## Art pipeline (Blender)
Blender 5 is installed (`blender` on the PATH). Generator scripts in `tools/blender/` build models in code; run them with `blender --background --python <script> -- <out_dir> [preview_dir]`.
- Map generators (`tools/*_layout.py`) write Rojo map folders; `tools/maplib.py` has the shared helpers (parts, blocks, cylinders, flat triangles, straight walls with doors and windows, slabs with holes, round walls and floors from exact pieces, ladders, parts on a round wall, lights, text, particles, writing the folder) and `tools/mapcheck.py` the checks every map must pass (flicker, windows, ladders, reach). Designs not in the game yet live in `tools/designs/` (none right now). Three maps were ported from their canvases' own design data because their generators never made it into the repo: `tools/designs/metro_v1.json` (`tools/metro_layout.py`), `tools/designs/market_v1.json` (`tools/market_layout.py`) and `tools/designs/playroom_v1.json` (`tools/playroom_layout.py`); `tools/designs/canvas_import.py` turns that data back into parts (styles, sizes, rotations, flags including balls and wedges, motions; `place` adds one) and each generator adds back what the export loses (names, materials, attributes, sign text, ladder trusses), and `tools/designs/canvas_export.py` exports a design or a map generator for its 3D design canvas (`python3 tools/designs/canvas_export.py rooftops_layout build/rooftops.json`; it also carries train cars and doors, `TrainCar`/`TrainDoor`, and the canvas's `train`, and `ring_segment` takes a `center`).
- `make_lobby.py`: the old lobby island (replaced by the Night Market in 0.52.0, unused; it also writes a `src/tools/LobbyData.luau` nothing reads any more). 118 objects, one mesh each (about 36k triangles total), colored by a one-row palette texture (`LobbyPalette.png`, flip-proof); each object has its own mesh named after it (so imported pieces are named predictably); glow pieces separate; sign text is data, not mesh. Studs, Z up, front faces -Y. Writes `assets_src/lobby/UltimateTagLobby.blend`/`.fbx`, `src/tools/LobbyData.luau`, and with a preview dir, renders (`assets_src/lobby/previews/`). Also runs from Blender's Text Editor.
- `make_character.py`, `smooth_body.py`: earlier character experiments (unused).
