# Ultimate Tag

A simple, classic-looking Roblox tag game. Each round some players are It: left-click to tag someone and pass it on, and don't be It when the timer runs out, or you're out. Anyone can swing to shove people around, and speed carries: falls, ramps and slides build it, and a fast hit sends people flying. Rounds move to new maps until one player is left. The code lives in files here and syncs into Roblox Studio with [Rojo](https://rojo.space).

## First-time setup
1. Install [Rokit](https://github.com/rojo-rbx/rokit).
2. Double-click `scripts/setup.command`. It installs the tools and the Rojo Studio plugin, and builds `UltimateTag.rbxlx`.

## Every day
1. Double-click `scripts/start.command`. It opens VS Code and Studio and starts `rojo serve`.
2. In Studio, open **Plugins > Rojo**, set the port to **34874**, and click **Connect**.
3. Edit files in `src/` and they update live in Studio.
4. To play a real game you need at least 2 players: **Test > Clients and Servers**, 2 or more players, **Start**. For movement testing alone, press Play and switch to Sandbox in the dev panel.

## Dev panel
In any Studio playtest, press the **`** key (left of 1) to open the dev panel. Players never see it.
- **Game**: switch Elimination / Sandbox, start a game now, skip the timer, set the time left or round length, make yourself It or a Runner, freeze, respawn.
- **Maps**: every map with its player range. **Play** loads it and puts you on it, **Play (all)** brings everyone, **Next round** makes the next game round use it. Unload, go to the lobby, show spawn points.
- **Movement**: sliders for the main movement numbers, applied instantly. **Copy changes** lists what you changed (also printed to Output) so it can go into `src/shared/Config.luau`; changes are lost when the playtest stops.
- **Debug**: movement readout (speed, current move, state), fly + noclip (WASD, E up, Q down, Shift faster), teleport key (**T** jumps to the mouse), respawn.

To use it in a live server, add your UserId to `Config.DevPanel.UserIds`.

## Progression and rewards
Players earn coins and XP by playing, level up, and collect cosmetics (trails, tag effects, titles, victory celebrations) from the daily shop, the Mystery crate, levels, login streaks and achievements. Rarity decides the look: Common is one color, Rare two colors or a pattern, Epic a gradient, Legendary adds effects, and Iridescent (0.05% from crates) gets holo colors, a serial number and a shout-out to the whole server. Daily and weekly quests, achievements and a 7-day login streak track themselves; players claim the rewards by hand.

The menu buttons on the left of the screen open the **Shop** (today's items, plus a Coin shop for Robux), the **Locker** (equip cosmetics and preview them on your own character in a 3D scene), **Quests**, **Rewards** (the streak and your crates) and **Leaders** (leaderboards and your stats).

To test it, open the dev panel (**`**) and use the **Progress** tab: give coins, XP and crates (open them in Rewards), unlock every cosmetic (then try them in the Locker), finish your quests, and move the calendar forward a day or a week to test the streak, new quests and the shop changing. Progress is saved only with **Game Settings > Security > Enable Studio Access to API Services** turned on (and the place published); otherwise it lasts for the session.

Robux items (VIP, 2x Coins, coin packs) are built in but off (they show "Soon"): create them on the Roblox website and put their IDs in `Config.Monetization`.

## Trying the School map
With Rojo connected the map is already in Studio (`ServerStorage > Maps > School`). Press Play, open the dev panel (**`**), go to **Maps**, and press **Play** next to School. Its layout is generated by `tools/school_layout.py`: change it, run `python3 tools/school_layout.py`, and Rojo syncs the result.

The School has two floors round an atrium. Ways up and down to try: the grand stair in the atrium, the gym bleachers and springboard, the two corner stairwells, the collapsed floor in the cafeteria, the hole in the science lab floor (with the map's one ladder back up), the bridge from the balcony to the library mezzanine, and jumping off the balcony, mezzanine or running-track railings. To climb a ladder, walk into it; jump to let go.

## Trying the Clocktower
The same way: dev panel (**`**) > **Maps** > **Play** next to The Clocktower. Its layout is generated by `tools/clocktower_layout.py`. It has four levels: the pit (jump up the gears and take the ladder beside the top one, loop the escapement drum, slide under its drive shaft), the Works behind the pit wall (spiral ramps up), the gallery (ride the pendulum across, take the stairs up), and the clock deck (jump onto the spinning crown wheel).

## Trying the Rooftop District
The same way: dev panel (**`**) > **Maps** > **Play** next to Rooftop District (in a real game it comes up with 8 to 12 players). Its layout is generated by `tools/rooftops_layout.py`. Nine rooftops on three heights round a central hub: wall-run along the billboards over the alleys, slide under the duct, ride the swinging platform, take the launch vents, scaffold steps, fire escapes and ladders up, and jump the gaps down. There's no ground: fall off during a round and you're out of the game.

## Trying the Metro
Dev panel (**`**) > **Maps** > **Play** next to The Metro (in a real game it comes up with 2 to 6 players). Its layout is generated by `tools/metro_layout.py` from the design data in `tools/designs/metro_v1.json`. Wait on the green side for the train, get on before the doors shut, ride the loop and get pushed out on the pink side. Stay off the tracks when the edge lights flash: the train kills you (out of the game during a round). Slide under the sheds, wall-run or slide-jump the gaps at the works, slide through the staff room. The tunnels are shut off by invisible barriers: only the train's riders go through.

## Trying Tag Mart
Dev panel (**`**) > **Maps** > **Play** next to Tag Mart (in a real game it comes up with 2 to 6 players). Its layout is generated by `tools/market_layout.py` from the design data in `tools/designs/market_v1.json`. Run the shelf tops (up from a pallet, a produce bin, the freezer island or checkout belt 2), wall-run the sale banners across the promo aisle, take the belts for a speed boost, and loop the back: under the deli hatch, through the kitchen and stockroom, up the ladder to the office.

## Trying the Playroom
Dev panel (**`**) > **Maps** > **Play** next to Playroom (in a real game it comes up with 2 to 6 players). Its layout is generated by `tools/playroom_layout.py` from the design data in `tools/designs/playroom_v1.json`. Jump the spinner's bar, walk up the books onto the bed and wall-run the wardrobe onto the desk, climb the castle and take the slide (jump at the very end of its lip to land on the toy box), step on the jack-in-the-box to be thrown onto the gatehouse, and slide through the train tunnel.

## Map checks
Every map in rotation, and the lobby, must pass `python3 tools/mapcheck.py` (it's in the commit checks): no flickering surfaces, no paper-thin or inside-out parts, nothing cutting into windows, no ladder floating off its wall, and every walkable area reachable from the spawns and back. The map generators run it before they write.

## The lobby
The lobby is the Night Market: a rainy neon night market where you spawn on the plaza facing the three leaderboards (GLOBAL LEADERS, THIS SESSION, RICHEST PLAYERS). Walk east under the arch down Market Street and press **E** at a shop stall to open the Shop, eat at the food court or the noodle bar, or go south through the gate into Training Alley for a lap of six stations (slide, gap jumps, wall run, launch pad, ladder, tag dummies). It's generated by `tools/lobby_layout.py` into `src/lobby`, and Rojo puts it in the game as `Workspace.Lobby`: change the generator, not Studio (Studio edits to the lobby are overwritten the next time Rojo syncs).

For saved wins and the global and richest leaderboards, publish the place and turn on **Game Settings > Security > Enable Studio Access to API Services**.
