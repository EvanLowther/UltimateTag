- 0.57.1: Momentum can't run away any more. Holding sprint, slide and jump used to chain slide, jump, hop and slide again, each slide multiplying your speed by 1.25 again (a hop or two outlasted the dead-slide time) and each faster slide-jump going higher (`JumpLift`), up to 90. Now a slide's boost only ever takes you up to a fresh slide's speed from a sprint (sprint speed x `StartMultiplier`; faster carried speed is kept, never multiplied), and every landing keeps only `Momentum.LandKeep` (0.6) of the speed you carried above your run speed, also when the jump is held and you bounce straight back up (`LandRebound`), so chained hops and slide-jumps wind back down to a sprint in a few landings. Falls still build speed (the drop's `LandCarry` is added after the loss). Untested in game.

- 0.56.5: Fix: the code-built animations (slide, crouch, tag swing, wall run, the victories' poses) and the joint steps laid over them (the first-person arm lift and wall hand, the victories' arm reach on the katana and trophy) did nothing, in Studio and live servers. Roblox's Avatar Joint Upgrade (rolling out to every game left on `StarterPlayer.AvatarJointUpgrade` Default since May 2026) gives characters AnimationConstraints instead of Motor6Ds, and that code only looked for Motor6Ds, so it found no joints (only the hip drop showed, so a slide looked like a crouch). New `client/Joints` finds a body part's joint of either kind (an AnimationConstraint's attachments stand in for a Motor6D's C0 and C1) and looks again if the rig's joints are swapped after it spawns; AnimationBuilder, FirstPersonArms and the victories' Kit use it.
